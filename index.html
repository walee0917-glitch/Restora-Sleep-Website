A helpful starting point

Sleep is personal. Your routine can be, too.

Sleep timing tools can help with planning, but they cannot predict your sleep stages or replace healthy sleep habits.

Keep a steady schedule

Try to keep your wake-up time consistent across the week when your circumstances allow.

Make your room comfortable

Reduce disruptive light and noise, and choose a temperature that feels comfortable.

Notice what affects you

Track caffeine, stress, naps and evening routines to see what patterns emerge.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about our tools and the information on this site.

Are sleep cycles exactly 90 minutes?

No. A 90-minute cycle is a simplified average. Sleep cycles vary across the night and from person to person.

Does the calculator guarantee I will wake refreshed?

No. It provides estimated times based on assumptions. Sleep quality depends on many factors, including total sleep, health and regularity.

Is my sleep tracker data uploaded?

The tracker stores entries in your browser's local storage on the device you use. Clearing browser data may remove those entries.
