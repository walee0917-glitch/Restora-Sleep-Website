Sleep Cycle Calculator

Estimate possible bedtimes or wake-up times using a cycle length you choose. Use the results as a planning aid, not a guarantee.

Important: sleep cycles are not fixed 90-minute blocks. This calculator uses a simplified estimate and cannot identify your actual sleep stages. Adults generally need at least 7 hours of sleep; do not routinely reduce sleep to fit a calculator result.
Advertisement space

How this calculator works

Choose whether you have a target wake-up time or bedtime. The tool counts backward or forward in several assumed cycle lengths and includes the time you estimate it takes to fall asleep.

Use the result thoughtfully

Prioritize enough total sleep and a schedule you can maintain. If you regularly feel unrefreshed despite allowing enough time for sleep, consider speaking with a healthcare professional.
