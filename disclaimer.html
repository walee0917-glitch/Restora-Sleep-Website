Health & Content Disclaimer

Information on Restora Sleep is provided for general educational purposes.

Not medical advice

Content, quizzes, calculators and tracking tools on this website are not medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. They are not a substitute for consultation with a qualified healthcare professional.

Calculator limitations

Sleep cycle length varies among individuals and across the night. Our calculator uses a simplified user-selected estimate and cannot measure sleep stages, diagnose a sleep disorder or guarantee that a suggested time will make you feel refreshed.

Seek professional guidance

If you have persistent difficulty sleeping, excessive daytime sleepiness, loud snoring, breathing pauses during sleep or another health concern, speak with a qualified healthcare professional. Seek urgent help for urgent medical symptoms.

Accuracy and external links

We aim to keep information clear and current, but we cannot guarantee that every item is complete or suitable for every person. External links are provided for convenience and do not imply endorsement.
